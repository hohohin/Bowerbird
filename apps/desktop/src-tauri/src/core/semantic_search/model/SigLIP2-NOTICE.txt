The separate FP32 text and vision ONNX exports were published by Hugging Face
staff in ONNX Community. The model card identifies the Google checkpoint above
as the base model. The converted files were uploaded on 2025-02-21 at 17:45 UTC,
after Google's 04:03 UTC revision. Google's history shows a single weight upload
before this conversion, followed by processor, config and README updates.
The pinned Google and ONNX Community tokenizer blobs are byte-identical.
